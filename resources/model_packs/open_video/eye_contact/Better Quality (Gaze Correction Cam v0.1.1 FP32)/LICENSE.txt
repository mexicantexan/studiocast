Notes:
- This pack represents an FP32 ONNX export of the upstream FLX v0.1.1 left/right warping models.
- FP32 is the most compatible precision (CPU EP and GPU EP).
- This pack depends on the dlib 68-point landmark predictor pack (trained on iBUG 300-W).
- StudioCast does not ship the model binaries in git.
