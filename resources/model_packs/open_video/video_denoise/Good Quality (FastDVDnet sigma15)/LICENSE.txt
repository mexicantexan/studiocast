FastDVDnet Video Denoising Model (sigma=15)

Upstream source:
- https://github.com/m-tassano/fastdvdnet

License:
- MIT (see upstream LICENSE)

Paper:
- "FastDVDnet: Towards Real-Time Deep Video Denoising Without Flow Estimation" (Tassano et al.)
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01361

Notes:
- StudioCast does not ship the model binary in git.
- This directory only contains metadata used by StudioCast's model installer/runtime.
- This pack represents a sigma preset (strength baseline). StudioCast may still map user "strength" to sigma at runtime.
